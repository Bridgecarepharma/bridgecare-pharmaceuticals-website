# Bridgecare Website v6.5.0 — Storefront & Shipping Refresh

## Storefront improvements
- Added a prominent free-delivery announcement bar.
- Added a desktop/mobile "Shop by health need" section inspired by modern ecommerce category navigation.
- Added a cart progress message that tells customers how many packs remain before free delivery.
- Preserved the existing Paystack checkout, cart, admin, health centre, reviews and mobile-admin API.

## New delivery policy
Free nationwide delivery applies automatically from 4 packs/products.

For orders below 4 packs:
- Lagos Mainland — ₦3,500
- Lagos Island — ₦5,000
- South West Nigeria — ₦4,500
- South South Nigeria — ₦5,000
- South East Nigeria — ₦5,000
- Northern Nigeria — ₦5,500

The checkout exposes Lagos Mainland and Lagos Island as separate selectable delivery areas and applies the four-pack free-delivery threshold server-side.
