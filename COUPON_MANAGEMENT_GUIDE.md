# Coupon Management Guide

Open `https://bridgecarepharmang.com/admin/marketing/coupons` after signing in.

- **Percentage:** enter `10` for 10%.
- **Fixed amount:** enter the discount in naira.
- **Free delivery:** the value field is ignored.
- Leave product boxes unchecked to apply the coupon to every product.
- A coupon with prior redemptions is archived rather than physically deleted.
- Coupon totals are always recalculated on the server before Paystack opens.
