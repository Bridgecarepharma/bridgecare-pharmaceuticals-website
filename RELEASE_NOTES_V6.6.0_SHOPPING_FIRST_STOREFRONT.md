# Bridgecare Pharmaceuticals Website v6.6.0 — Shopping-First Storefront

This release builds on v6.5.2 and adapts strong ecommerce patterns observed on Ome’s Beauty Mart to Bridgecare’s healthcare identity.

## Improvements
- Adds a desktop shopping-category navigation row beneath the main header.
- Moves purchasable products higher on the homepage so customers can shop immediately.
- Adds a three-part commerce reassurance band for free delivery, payment choice, and WhatsApp support.
- Keeps Shop by Health Need as a fast category-discovery experience.
- Rebuilds the Products page into a clearer shop catalogue with search, category chips, delivery incentive, prices and Add to Cart.
- Preserves Bridgecare’s pharmaceutical visual identity rather than copying another retailer’s branding.
- Preserves Lagos Pay on Delivery and Paystack checkout.
- Corrects the legacy store fallback free-shipping threshold to 4 packs.

## Delivery
Free nationwide delivery: 4+ packs. Smaller orders use the configured regional shipping zones.
