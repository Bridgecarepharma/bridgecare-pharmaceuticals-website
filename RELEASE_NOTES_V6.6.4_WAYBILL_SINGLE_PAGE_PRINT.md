# Bridgecare v6.6.4 — Waybill single-page print fix

- Keeps the delivery waybill at exactly 100 mm × 150 mm.
- Hides the normal website header, footer, floating Call/WhatsApp controls, cart drawer and chat widgets during printing.
- Pins the waybill to the printable page so unrelated site content cannot spill onto extra pages.
- Preserves the order barcode, sender details and existing waybill content.
- Designed to produce a single print-preview page on standard browser printing and 100 × 150 mm label media.
