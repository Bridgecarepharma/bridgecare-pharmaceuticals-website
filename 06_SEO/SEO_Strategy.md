# SEO Starter Strategy

## Principles
- Answer genuine health questions clearly.
- Build topic clusters around each Knowledge Centre.
- Link educational articles to relevant ingredient and product pages without aggressive sales language.
- Use medically responsible titles and descriptions.
- Add Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Product structured data where appropriate.

## Initial topic clusters
PCOS and inositol; nerve health and B vitamins; sickle cell wellness; omega-3 and daily nutrition; blood sugar lifestyle education.

## Quality controls
No mass publishing without review. Every article should have an author, reviewer, last-reviewed date, evidence label and references where appropriate.
