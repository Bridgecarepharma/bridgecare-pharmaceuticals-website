# Bridgecare v3.2.1 — Selectable Shipping Zones

- Displays all active delivery zones as radio-button choices during checkout.
- Shows the current charge for Lagos, South West, South East and Northern State.
- Automatically displays free delivery when the configured pack threshold is reached.
- Keeps the chosen delivery zone synchronized with the selected delivery state.
- Validates the delivery zone and state on the server before initializing Paystack.
- Disabled zones are automatically hidden from checkout.
