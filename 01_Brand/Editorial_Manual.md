# Bridgecare Brand & Editorial Manual

## Voice
Professional, clear, caring, respectful and confident.

## Avoid
Aggressive selling, fear-based language, exaggerated claims, unsupported certainty, unnecessary medical jargon and sensational headlines.

## Preferred product language
- Supports...
- Helps maintain...
- Formulated to...
- May contribute to...
- Traditionally used for... where appropriate
- As part of a healthy lifestyle...

## Prohibited language without approved evidence and authorisation
- Cure
- Miracle
- Guaranteed result
- No side effects
- Works for everyone
- Prevents or treats a disease

## Evidence labels
- **Established:** supported by broad scientific consensus.
- **Emerging:** promising research, but not conclusive.
- **Traditional use:** longstanding use without definitive clinical proof.

## Standard article structure
1. Title
2. Hero image
3. Reading time
4. Author or editorial team
5. Last reviewed date
6. Evidence level
7. Key takeaways
8. Table of contents
9. Main educational content
10. Practical wellness tips
11. FAQs
12. Related articles
13. Related products
14. References
15. Disclaimer

## Golden rule
Every product should teach. Every article should help. Every interaction should build trust.
