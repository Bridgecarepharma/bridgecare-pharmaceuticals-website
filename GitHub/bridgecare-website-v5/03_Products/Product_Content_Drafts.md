# Bridgecare Product Content Drafts

## Aspivit®
A comprehensive multivitamin and omega-3 supplement formulated to support daily nutritional needs. Final ingredient quantities, directions and warnings must be inserted from the approved label.

## AsFenositol®
An inositol-based supplement designed to support women's wellness as part of a healthy lifestyle and appropriate clinical care. It must not be presented as a cure for PCOS or infertility.

## Globivida®
A targeted nutritional-support formulation intended for people living with sickle cell disease. It must be positioned as complementary support and never as a replacement for prescribed treatment.

## Bridgecare Herbal Bitter Tea®
A botanical tea inspired by traditional use. The site should distinguish traditional knowledge from established clinical evidence and avoid detox, cure or disease-treatment claims unless specifically authorised.

## RenerveVitaX®
A nutritional supplement formulated with nutrients that support healthy nerve function. Avoid claims that it treats neuropathy or eliminates nerve pain unless the claim is specifically approved.
