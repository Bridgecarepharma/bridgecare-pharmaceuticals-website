# v6.4.17 — Mobile Storefront Declutter

## Goal
Reduce visual crowding on the Bridgecare homepage on phones without changing the desktop storefront or commerce/tracking logic.

## Changes
- Simplified the mobile hero to one primary **Shop products** CTA.
- Hid duplicate hero WhatsApp and assurance pills on small screens.
- Reduced the mobile hero/product-family artwork height.
- Converted the four trust rows into a compact 2×2 mobile grid.
- Reduced mobile section spacing and homepage product-card height.
- Hid repeated benefit chips on homepage product cards on phones; detailed benefits remain on product pages.
- Kept only one floating mobile contact action (WhatsApp); the floating phone button is hidden on phones.
- Hid the separate Tawk chat bubble on screens up to 600px to prevent duplicate chat controls.

## Unchanged
- Desktop layout and CTAs.
- Product pages and product information.
- Cart, Buy Now, checkout and Paystack flows.
- Meta/marketing analytics and purchase tracking.
- Tawk chat on tablet/desktop widths above 600px.
