# Health Centre CMS Guide

1. Sign in at `/admin/login`.
2. Open **Health Centre** in the admin navigation.
3. Open **Categories** to review or add article categories.
4. Click **New article**.
5. Enter the title, summary and article body.
6. Add optional tags, featured-image URL and SEO fields.
7. Save as **Draft** while reviewing, then change the status to **Published**.
8. Published articles appear automatically at `/health-centre` and in `sitemap.xml`.

## Featured images

Use a publicly accessible HTTPS image URL. Add descriptive alt text for accessibility and search visibility.

## Editorial standard

Articles should be clear, responsible and evidence-informed. Avoid claims that a product diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents disease unless the exact claim is lawfully authorised. Encourage readers to consult qualified healthcare professionals where appropriate.
